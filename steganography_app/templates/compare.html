How Each Technique Works
LSB (Least Significant Bit)

LSB replaces the least significant bits of pixel values with message bits:

  • Highest capacity (can use multiple color channels)
  • Fast encoding and decoding
  • More susceptible to image modifications
DCT (Discrete Cosine Transform)

DCT works in the frequency domain by modifying image coefficients:

  • Better resistance to compression
  • Moderate capacity
  • More computationally intensive
DWT (Discrete Wavelet Transform)

DWT decomposes image into frequency sub-bands:

  • High robustness against attacks
  • Good balance of capacity and quality
  • Preserves image quality better
